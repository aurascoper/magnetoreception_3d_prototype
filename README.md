# 3D Magnetoreception, Biomagnetic Walk Null Explorer & Multiphysics-GLMM Interface (Prototype v2.2)

Independent hypothesis-first prototype repository (`feat/magnetoreception-3d-interface`) developed separately from `aurascoper/oklo_shareable_portal` and `aurascoper/Biofilms` so that radiation-melanin redox responses, candidate geomagnetic sensing mechanisms, engineered magnetic nanoparticle actuation, multiphysics biofilm transport/rheology, and hierarchical statistical inference can be investigated with explicit epistemic tags (`OBSERVED`, `OBSERVED_IN_VITRO`, `MODELED`, `HYPOTHESIZED`, `UNRESOLVED_TRANSDUCTION`, `ENGINEERED`, `CONTEXT-DEPENDENT`, `QUARANTINED`).

## Central Falsification Principle: Selection vs. Movement & Model Coupling vs. Biological Synchronization

An outward shift in the surviving biomass distribution does **not** establish outward movement (`radiotropism` / `magnetotropism`). It can arise because parcels near the source axis are preferentially removed by position-dependent mortality:

- **Null `H0-A` (No Active Directional Movement) vs. Null `H0-B` (Selection Imitates Movement)**: In `aurascoper/Biofilms` `cpm-sp-v1` (execution source `841d052`, configuration hash `eb090aa3c0a971776a6e262713eb0cf5c130b53cf2b8d7720ad93dec19f50b01`, Growth OFF, 256 seeds `42..297`, `N=40`, 6 parcels/species, MCS 100), axial dose-rate mortality removes `5,511` of `10,752` parcels in `survival_only` and `combined` (`0` in `baseline` and `phase_only`). This shifts the surviving population's mean radius outward for all 7 species (up to `+0.0493 r/R [0.0289, 0.0704]` in *S. oneidensis*, matching the initial radius of those same survivors `+0.0538 r/R [0.0327, 0.0740]`), while **every identified-parcel displacement interval `delta_r_i = r_i(t_1) - r_i(t_0)` spans zero** (`+0.0011` to `-0.0052 r/R`). In the 8 control seeds (`7000..7007`), `fixed_survival` removes `18 to 23` of `42` parcels per seed with **exact `0` displacement**.
- **Phase-Locking & 3 Required Counterfactual Controls (`15 of 16` Growth-On vs. `0 of 256` Growth-Off)**: In `cpm-sp-growth-v1` (Growth ON, 16 seeds `42..57`, MCS 200, nutrient/biomass budgets closed to `7.2e-9 < 1e-6`), division fills the lattice into a single connected component (`1` component, `0` isolates) and explicit model coupling (`K = 0.5/MCS`) locks **`15 of 16` seeds** (Kuramoto order parameter `R(200) = 0.994`, `98.2%` median frequency-spread reduction vs. the exact `K = 0` uncoupled replay counterfactual; Seed 44 fails the `0.5 rad` range bound at `0.755` / `0.848` rad). Without growth (`256` seeds, MCS 100), the fragmented contact graph (`~11.3` components, `5.2` isolates) produces **`0 of 256` seeds** locked (`R(100) = 0.411`). All phase parameters are synthetic and establish model-coupling robustness, **not** biological synchronization. Three controls are enforced:
  1. **Control 1 (Uncoupled `K = 0` Replay)**: Replays the exact recorded parcel lineage and initial phases with `K = 0`.
  2. **Control 2 (Phase-Randomized & Cross-Seed Surrogate)**: Quantifies apparent phase concentration `R(t)` from finite-sample chance, shared geometry, or common external forcing.
  3. **Control 3 (Preregistered Biological Benchmark vs. Independent-Oscillator Null)**: Requires live-cell reporter time-series to exceed an uncoupled-oscillator + lineage-inheritance null before any biological synchronization claim is permitted.

## Revised 5-Row Planned-Contrast Experimental Matrix (15 Cells vs. 24-Cell Full Crossed Factorial)

Every row is crossed with three fungal genotypes (**Wild-Type Melanized**, **Melanin-Deficient / Albino**, and **Complemented Rescue**), yielding **15 condition-by-genotype combinations** (a planned-contrast design focused on radiation-coupled magnetic manipulations). A **fully crossed 24-cell design** (`2 Radiation states x 4 Magnetic-field states x 3 Genotypes`) additionally tests rotated (`+90 deg`), reversed (`180 deg`), and suppressed (`<0.1 uT`) magnetic fields under sham radiation to separate general magnetic effects from radiation-dependent magnetic effects:

| Row | Radiation Condition | Magnetic-Field Manipulation | Crossed Genotypes (3 per Row) | Primary Contrast |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | Sham radiation (`0 Gy`) | Baseline field (`50 uT`); sham-coil control | WT, Melanin-Deficient, Complemented Rescue | Baseline growth, survival, and directionality |
| **2** | Radiation (`+grad I_gamma`) | Baseline field (`50 uT`); matched sham-coil control | WT, Melanin-Deficient, Complemented Rescue | Radiation x melanin-genotype response (separating selection `H0-B` from movement `H0-A`) |
| **3** | Radiation (`+grad I_gamma`) | Rotate field orientation (`+90 deg`) at fixed magnitude (`50 uT`) | WT, Melanin-Deficient, Complemented Rescue | Orientation-dependent response orthogonal to fixed radiation gradient |
| **4** | Radiation (`+grad I_gamma`) | Reverse field direction (`180 deg`, `+B -> -B`) at fixed magnitude (`50 uT`) | WT, Melanin-Deficient, Complemented Rescue | Polarity-sensitive (magnetite torque `tau = m x B`) vs. polarity-insensitive (radical-pair inclination compass) response |
| **5** | Radiation (`+grad I_gamma`) | Suppress field toward prespecified near-zero target (`<0.1 uT`); matched sham coils | WT, Melanin-Deficient, Complemented Rescue | Field-dependent response vs. radiation-only effects |

## Integrated 4-Layer Multiphysics & Hierarchical GLMM Architecture (`Layers A, B, C, D`)

1. **Layer A: Hydrodynamics & Reactive Transport (Darcy-Brinkman-Stokes)**: Couples Navier-Stokes free flow and Darcy-Brinkman porous biofilm flow with nutrient/oxygen/redox-mediator reactive transport, enforcing mass-conservation and grid-convergence checks (`< 1e-6` residual; citing Kim & Yao 2026 `arXiv:2610.01565`).
2. **Layer B: Microbial Kinetics & Viscoelastic Biofilm Rheology**: Separates species biomass balance (`dX_i/dt + div(u*X_i) = div(D_i*grad(X_i)) + mu_i*X_i - d_i*X_i + a_i - e_i`) and Oldroyd-B viscoelastic EPS stress relaxation (`G'`, `G''`, detachment under shear) from active transport, requiring independent rheometric calibration rather than circular fitting from colony images (citing Charlton et al. 2019 `PMC6707926`; Li, Matous & Nerenberg 2022 `doi:10.1002/bit.28056`).
3. **Layer C: Falsifiable Magnetic & Radiation Hypotheses (`theta_i = 0`)**: Parameterizes any magnetic or radiation effect on growth (`mu_i = mu_{i,0} + theta_i * f(B, grad B, I_gamma)`) or directional transport (`v_bias,i = gamma_i * g(B, grad I_gamma)`) around an explicit null (`H_0: theta_i = 0, gamma_i = 0`; citing Dadachova et al. 2007 `doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0000457`; Maeda et al. 2012 `doi:10.1073/pnas.1118959109`; Rishabh et al. 2023 `arXiv:2312.06597`; Gassab et al. 2026 `arXiv:2605.00205`).
4. **Layer D: Hierarchical Generalized Linear Mixed Model (GLMM) Inference**: Models `g(E[Y_{ijk}(t)]) = beta_0 + beta_R*R + beta_B*B + beta_M*M + beta_RB*(R*B) + beta_BM*(B*M) + beta_RBM*(R*B*M) + b_batch + b_culture + f(t)`, keeping simulation seeds, biological cultures, and image pixels distinct across separated endpoint families (Gaussian LMM for displacement/modulus; Poisson/NegBin GLMM for attachment/detachment counts; Binomial GLMM for parcel survival; longitudinal covariance mixed model for repeated rheology; compositional mixed model with absolute abundance for multispecies fractions; citing Lee, Green & Ryan 2017 `arXiv:1709.06288`).
