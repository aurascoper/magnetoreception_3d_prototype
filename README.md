# 3D Magnetoreception, Melanin-Redox-Mitochondrial & Magnetogenetics Research Interface (Prototype v2.1)

Independent hypothesis-first prototype repository (`feat/magnetoreception-3d-interface`) developed separately from `aurascoper/oklo_shareable_portal` and `aurascoper/Biofilms` so that radiation-melanin redox responses, candidate geomagnetic sensing mechanisms, engineered magnetic nanoparticle actuation, and downstream mitochondrial signaling can be investigated with explicit epistemic tags (`OBSERVED`, `OBSERVED_IN_VITRO`, `MODELED`, `HYPOTHESIZED`, `UNRESOLVED_TRANSDUCTION`, `ENGINEERED`, `CONTEXT-DEPENDENT`, `QUARANTINED`).

## Core Epistemic Principle: Separating Energy Delivery vs. Directional Information

- **Radiation Dose Gradient (`grad I_gamma`)**: Acts as a candidate **energy / radiolytic redox input channel** (altering melanin ESR signals and driving 4x NADH reduction in vitro; Dadachova et al. 2007 *PLoS ONE* `doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0000457`).
- **Magnetic Flux Density Vector (`B`, `25 to 65 uT` geomagnetic or `5 to 250 mT` engineered)**: Acts as a **directional spin-chemistry or mechanical torque information channel**.
- **Inference Gate**: A colony plate image or metabolic assay alone does not establish that radiation or magnetic fields caused directional growth (`radiotropism` / `magnetotropism`). In `aurascoper/Biofilms` (`diagnostics/melanin_ensemble/radial_n40_p6_seeds42-297.csv`), the 256-seed, 4-arm ensemble (`full`, `no_rad`, `no_mel`, `null`) is a formal TOST equivalence null within `+/-0.05 r/R`, and Sandia National Laboratories (2024) reported no statistically significant difference in Cs-137 fungal growth experiments.

## Main Causal Gap Decomposition (`Propositions P1 -> P2 -> P3 -> P4`)

Evidence for one proposition in one chemical or biological system does not establish the next proposition in intact fungi:

1. **Proposition P1 (`OBSERVED` in vitro & melanized fungi)**: Melanin changes its ESR signal and electron-transfer (4x NADH reduction) properties under ionizing radiation, with radiation-associated growth/transcriptomic shifts reported in melanized fungi (Dadachova et al. 2007 *PLoS ONE* `doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0000457`; Robertson et al. 2012). This supports the radiation-response track; it does **not** establish a melanin-mediated magnetic compass.
2. **Proposition P2 (`OBSERVED_IN_VITRO` for cryptochrome / `MODELED` for flavin-superoxide / `HYPOTHESIZED` for melanin)**: Photoinduced flavin-tryptophan radical pairs in cryptochrome have experimentally demonstrated magnetic-field-sensitive reaction kinetics and quantum yields in vitro (Maeda et al. 2012 *PNAS* `doi:10.1073/pnas.1118959109`; Xu et al. 2021 *Nature*). Separately, Rishabh, Zadeh-Haghighi & Simon (2023, `arXiv:2312.06597`) and a 2024 *Nature Communications* study computationally model flavin-superoxide (`[FAD*- ... O2*-]`) radical pairs; Rishabh et al. explicitly state that their model does not establish the mechanism in vivo. Magnetic modulation of melanin radical chemistry remains an untested hypothesis.
3. **Proposition P3 (`UNRESOLVED_TRANSDUCTION_CHAIN`)**: Even where spin-dependent chemical reaction yields are altered, the downstream biological transduction chain through ROS and mitochondrial signaling to cellular polarity requires independent causal evidence at each step (Gassab et al. 2026 `arXiv:2605.00205`; Lam & Malkemper 2026 *Physiol. Rev.* `doi:10.1152/physrev.00032.2025`).
4. **Proposition P4 (`CONTEXT-DEPENDENT / UNRESOLVED`)**: Reproducible directional fungal hyphal growth (`radiotropism` / `magnetotropism`) remains unresolved between early Chernobyl isolate reports (Zhdanova et al. 2004), the Sandia National Laboratories (2024) Cs-137 experimental null, and the `aurascoper/Biofilms` 256-seed 4-arm computational null.

## Sequentially Gated, Preregistered Experiment Sequence (`Gates A -> B -> C -> D`)

Before interpreting any gate, preregister the primary outcome (`delta r/R` or circular mean tip angle `theta_tip`), the minimum biologically meaningful effect (`MEOI = 0.05 r/R` or `>= 15 deg`, with two one-sided TOST equivalence tests at `alpha = 0.05`), exclusion rules, replicate structure, sham-coil + 3-axis magnetometer verification at the sample plane, and Holm-Bonferroni multiplicity correction:

- **Gate A (`Radiation x Melanin Genetics`)**: Compare `WT Melanized`, `Albino Mutant (pks1Delta/lac1Delta)`, and `Complemented Rescue` under irradiated and sham conditions to establish whether the response depends on melanin.
- **Gate B (`Magnetic-Field Rotation & Suppression at Fixed Radiation Geometry`)**: Keep radiation geometry, dose, spectrum, culture conditions, and temperature (`+/-0.05 C`) fixed while rotating `B` (`+90 deg`) or suppressing `B` (`<0.1 uT`) with active double-wrapped antiparallel sham coils and independent sample-plane magnetometer measurements.
- **Gate C (`Melanin x Magnetic-Field Factorial`)**: Test whether field-dependent responses persist in `Albino` mutants or require `WT` / `Complemented Rescue`, discriminating a melanin-dependent route (**Route A**) from cryptochrome or flavin-superoxide chemistry (**Route B**).
- **Gate D (`Temporal Ordering & Pathway Intervention`)**: Measure ROS/redox indicators alongside hyphal-tip curvature (`t_mito < t_tip`) and test whether a controlled pathway intervention abolishes the directional response without independently preventing normal growth.
